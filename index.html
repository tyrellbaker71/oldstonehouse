A home away from home, set in leafy Kloof

Welcome to The Old Stone House Guesthouse, where comfort, convenience and warm hospitality come together.

Situated in the leafy suburb of Kloof, KwaZulu-Natal, we offer welcoming accommodation for business travellers, holidaymakers, families and guests looking for a peaceful place to stay.

With three unique accommodation options to suit different budgets and needs, we provide a comfortable home away from home, whether you are visiting for a night, enjoying a getaway or requiring accommodation for an extended period.

Guests stay with us for business, holidays, family trips, temporary visits to the area and longer work assignments.
